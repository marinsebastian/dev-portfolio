# Call-to-Action (CTA) & Conversion Optimization Expert Review Prompt

**Role:** Senior Growth Marketer & Conversion Rate Optimization (CRO) Specialist  
**Target Candidate:** Sebastian Marin — Full-Stack Developer Portfolio  
**Target Audience:** CEO, CTO, CFO, Lead Engineer at Geolabs Cloud  

## Review Objectives
Evaluate the portfolio's interactive affordances, call-to-action (CTA) visibility, micro-copy enticement, and user friction to ensure that 100% of executive reviewers are invited and driven to test the live tools (MapLibre PMTiles GIS, Voronoi Spatial Lab, Interactive CV, PHP Sync API).

---

## Key Review Dimensions

1. **CTA Clarity & Prominence:**
   - Are primary action buttons (`[Ver Portfolio Interactivo]`, `[Explorar Datos Censo 2024]`, `[Probar API]`, `[Descargar CV PDF]`) visually distinct with high contrast?
   - Do interactive tools have explicit "Try Live" affordances rather than passive text?

2. **Micro-Copy Enticement:**
   - Does the copy invite active exploration (e.g. *"Interactúa con los manzanos reales del Censo 2024 en Santa Cruz, Cochabamba y La Paz"*)?
   - Is friction minimized (no login, no complex setup)?

3. **Executive Conversion Funnel:**
   - How easily can a reviewer move from the Hero section to the GIS Map, Case Studies, and Contact options?
   - Are contact CTAs prominent and clear?
