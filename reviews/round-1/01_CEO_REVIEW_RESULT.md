## Executive Verdict

## First 10 Seconds

- **Understanding in 10 Seconds:** In the first 10 seconds, I immediately understand that Sebastian is a Systems Engineer (`Ingeniero de Sistemas`) and Full-Stack Developer specializing in web interfaces, APIs, spatial data, and Linux automation.
- **Authenticity Check:** The site looks like legitimate engineering software rather than a generic AI-generated template. The dark console aesthetic (`#0B0F17`) and telemetry tags give it an operational enterprise tone.
- **Tailoring Assessment:** The positioning statement (*"Construyo sistemas web que convierten datos, APIs, información espacial y procesos en herramientas operativas simples de usar"*) communicates business utility rather than over-tailored marketing fluff aimed only at Geolabs.

## What Made Me Doubt Him

- **Code Block Overload in Workflow Section:** Technical code snippets (Playwright, PHP cURL, Docker Compose) are useful for the CTO, but take up significant visual space on the main landing page.
